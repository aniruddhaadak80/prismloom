ADR-0005: Procedural Web Audio, no samples
Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-15
Decision: Audio tracks are synthesized oscillators (square/saw/triangle/sine) with step sequencers; no audio assets.
Rationale: Zero-key, zero-bandwidth, fully in-browser. Fits creative-coding vibe.
Consequences: Limited timbral richness vs samples; no licensing concerns.
